## 03 Concept (PT#1)
**Tag:** 01 — Business Concept
**What is SPLSH?** [DECK] A water-adventure brand in Boracay offering water activities for different interests, personalities, and experience levels.

**Our story (History)** [DECK]
> How wet? How fun?
Born in Boracay Island, Malay, Aklan, from the idea that the best memories leave people smiling, laughing, and wanting more.
A beach trip can become just another day by the shore, so SPLSH makes every visit more exciting.
SPLSH brings together the thrill of water sports, the freedom of the open sea, and the playful energy of friends and family.

**Mission [CONFIRM — AI draft derived from deck]**
To turn ordinary beach days into exciting, shared adventures by offering a wide range of water activities that are fun, accessible, and safe for every kind of traveler in Boracay.

**Vision** [DECK]
To become a recognizable water-adventure brand that makes exciting, accessible, and memorable recreation for everyone.

**Values** [DECK]: Adventure · Inclusivity · Convenience · Enjoyment · Connection · Memorable Experience

**Who we serve** [DECK]
- Primary: Young adults & students · Young professionals · Friends & adventure seekers
- Secondary: Families & couples · Domestic & international tourists · Recreational groups
- Lede: From accessible recreation to high-adrenaline sports and advanced water experiences — there's a SPLSH for every skill level.

**Why choose SPLSH?** [DECK]
1. A fun-filled experience — A youthful, energetic brand that turns ordinary beach days into exciting adventures and unforgettable shared experiences.
2. A distinct market identity — Diverse water activities, accessibility, and a playful personality that stand out in Boracay's tourism market.
