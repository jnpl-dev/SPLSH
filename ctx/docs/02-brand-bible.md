## Brand story (PT#1 → History)
Born in Boracay Island, Malay, Aklan, from the idea that the best memories leave people smiling, laughing,
and wanting more. A beach trip can become just another day by the shore, so SPLSH makes every visit more exciting.
SPLSH brings together the thrill of water sports, the freedom of the open sea, and the playful energy of friends and family.
